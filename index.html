ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Education with Strong Values

Saraswati Vidya Mandir Inter College, Afzalgarh (Bijnor), is affiliated with Vidya Bharati and is managed by Bharatiya Shiksha Samiti, Western Uttar Pradesh.

Our aim is to provide quality education while nurturing discipline, confidence, cultural values and social responsibility.

  • Moral and cultural values alongside education
  • Encouragement for sports and co-curricular activities
  • Focus on every student’s all-round development
  • A positive and supportive learning environment
